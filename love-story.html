How it began

A dream come true

It is sweet when a crush turns into something real, especially after time, distance, and a little bit of fate have done their work. There was a time when you were simply the person I admired from afar, someone I dreamed about while quietly hoping life would one day bring us closer. Those feelings were gentle, but they were real.

Years later, life brought us back together in the most beautiful way. The boy I once admired became the man I now love, laugh with, and dream beside. What once felt impossible became my favorite reality. Every moment with you reminds me that some hearts are not only meant to reconnect but to stay.

Since we have been together, we have built memories that are deeply precious to me. From simple dates and late conversations to quiet moments where love was felt more in the small details than in grand gestures, everything has felt warm and alive. Even the ordinary days feel special when I am with you.

I love you not only for the big romantic moments, but for the little things: your voice, your kindness, your patience, the way you make me feel safe and seen. Love is not only about a perfect story; it is about choosing each other again and again. And I choose you, every single day, with all my heart.

“In your arms, even the ordinary feels like a fairytale.”
